# Fable plan 2: highest-impact studies for a paper (2026-11-19)

Requested by the owner on 2026-11-19: aim for one paper per project, with research leading groups would see as a major contribution. Fable 5.2 was given a read-only brief. Its report is summarised below; the full text is in the Origins thread.

## Bottom line (Fable)
- **Odds.** A seminal result in days: under 1 in 100. The best bet is the **intervention ledger**:
  - a clean, defensible result: about 75%;
  - leading groups see it as a significant contribution: 10-15%;
  - "seminal": under 2%.
- **What it is.** Richert asked the field in 2018 to state the number of human interventions (doi:10.1038/s41467-018-07219-5) and gave no counts himself. The ledger supplies those counts: every major prebiotic RNA route, scored under the sealed rule v1, encoded twice blind, and checked by the authors.
- **The key insight.** The 0/27 count is already conceded. Ritson and Sutherland note a "consensus" that sequential addition and "occasional purification steps" are needed (doi:10.1021/jacs.2c07774). The finding is the **type** of break: never pH or temperature, always "the product of step n is not what step n+1 was tested with".

## Studies, ranked
1. **Intervention ledger (the paper).** 10-12 routes, about 60 hand-offs.
   - Added routes: Becker 2018 (PMC5765019, which claims "continuous synthesis"), Becker 2019, Ritson 2022, Xu/Powner 2020, the Krishnamurthy DAP papers, and Yi 2020.
   - Two blind encoders per route, with kappa reported.
   - Sealed predictions, written from abstracts only:
     - at most 3 new hand-offs pass;
     - every pass is within a single paper;
     - at least 90% of hard fails are F4 or F6, none is pH or temperature;
     - Becker 2018 fails at ribose entry.
   - Null: shuffle step order within each route.
   - Kill criteria: kappa below 0.6; the red team flips more than 20% of verdicts; a true one-pot sequence gets a hard fail.
   - Gated by red-team tasks #21 and #27. Time: 3-4 days.
   - Venue: ChemRxiv, then Astrobiology or Chem Eur J.
2. **QT45 contacts and variants from the open fitness landscape.**
   - Data: Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.13891380 (MIT licence, about 3 GB processed).
   - Sealed outputs: the top 10 non-Watson-Crick contacts, and 10 triple mutants predicted to beat wild type.
   - First check whether the paper already compares contacts.
   - Odds: a clear positive about 30%; significant 5-8% now, about 20% if a lab tests the predictions.
3. **Replication ledger.** One accounting of net growth for every RNA replication system. Fold it into study 1 as its S5 column.
